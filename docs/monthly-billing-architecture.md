# Monthly billing architecture

## Provider routing

- Parking-session PIX: Efí (already validated in Production).
- Monthly recurring PIX: Asaas Pix Automático.
- Monthly recurring card: Asaas recurring card/subscription.

## Pix Automático target flow

1. Customer enrolls in an internal monthly plan.
2. Star Carvalhos creates/uses the Asaas customer.
3. Star Carvalhos creates an Asaas Pix Automático authorization with `paymentCreationMode=SUBSCRIPTION`.
4. The initial QR code is shown to the customer.
5. Asaas webhook activates the authorization after consent/payment.
6. Internal subscription moves from `PENDING_ACTIVATION` to `ACTIVE` only after trusted provider confirmation.
7. Future monthly charges are generated by Asaas and mirrored into `monthly_billing_periods`/`payments` through idempotent webhook processing.
8. Failed/overdue periods enter the internal grace/suspension flow; the provider never directly grants parking coverage.

## Safety invariants

- Supabase remains the operational source of truth for parking coverage.
- Provider webhooks are idempotent and treated as at-least-once delivery.
- Provider resource IDs are persisted and unique where applicable.
- No provider confirmation automatically closes parking sessions.
- Existing Efí parking-session PIX flow must not be changed by this rollout.
- Do not enable Production recurring billing until account eligibility and webhook behavior are validated with a controlled canary.
